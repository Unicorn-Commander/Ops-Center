What it can do

  • Categorizes transactions and reconciles receipts
  • Drafts statements and tax packages
  • Traces report lines through an append-only journal

What waits for you

Material items and missing-receipt entries stop for human approval. Tax packages remain draft and do not file themselves.

The app self-hosts on your hardware and can use a local model, your hosted model API, or Unicorn Cloud.

Does data leave your network?

Self-hosted with a local model: no. Hosted models and connectors receive the records required for the task.
