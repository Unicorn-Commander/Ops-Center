What it can do

  • Records and transcribes meetings

What waits for you

Meeting-Ops proposes; the owning app disposes. Extracted action items are sent to Project-Ops as task proposals, and that push is off by default until an org turns it on. Nothing reaches a customer record until a human releases that summary, and it never overwrites another app’s state.

Where it runs

  • Local app
  • Unicorn Cloud
  • Self-host

Local means the Meeting-Ops appliance path, including local transcription and Ollama analysis.

Does data leave your network?

Self-hosted appliance: no. Hosted service: meeting data is processed in Unicorn Cloud.
