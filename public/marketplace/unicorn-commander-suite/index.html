What it can touch.
Where it runs.

What it can do

  • Entitles every Suite app under one Unicorn Commander account and one credit balance
  • Includes each app’s MCP tool surface, under the caller’s own role
  • BYOK tier calls model providers with keys you supply
  • Managed tier substitutes UC-hosted inference credits and priority support

What waits for you

The bundle grants entitlements; it does not widen them. Every member keeps the approval boundary printed on its own listing — Meeting-Ops still proposes rather than writes, and Project-Ops still holds AI-proposed work in a Triage queue until a human approves, routes, or rejects it. Buying the Suite never gives an agent a permission the owning app would refuse on its own.

Where it runs

  • Unicorn Cloud
  • BYOK

The Suite is a hosted entitlement across Unicorn Cloud, with BYOK as the tier that points the apps at your own model provider keys. Self-hosting is decided per member rather than by the bundle, because the members do not all ship the same way — see what self-hosting takes. What self-hosting takes.

Does data leave your network?

Depends on the tier and the member. On BYOK, model calls go to the provider whose keys you supply. On Managed, inference is processed on Magic Unicorn infrastructure. Each member listing states its own answer, and the bundle does not override it.

Source license

Per member — each listing states its own

The Suite is a commercial bundle, not a single codebase, so it has no license of its own and there is no blanket claim to make. Members with a listing in this catalog state their exact license there. Members without one have not had a license published yet.
