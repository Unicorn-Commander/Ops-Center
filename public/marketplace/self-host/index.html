Moving to your hardware changes the ground, not the contents: same identity, same gateway, same MCP surface, seated somewhere else. This page is the honest inventory of what that costs you.

01

What ships, and under what license

04 of 05 products are self-hostable today. Each states its own license. There is no blanket claim on this page because there is no blanket truth: four of these are Apache-2.0, and Center Deep Research's core remains AGPL-3.0 because it is built on SearXNG.

02

What does not change

This is the argument, and it matters more than the count above.

Identity

The same Keycloak realm and SSO model. Your users, your realm, your session policy.

Model gateway

The same gateway contract. Point it at your own hardware, your own keys, or Unicorn Cloud, per app.

MCP surface

The same tools, under the same role-based permissions. Agents do not get a private back door when you self-host.

Credits and metering

Still there, still auditable. On your hardware it measures your own consumption instead of billing it.
